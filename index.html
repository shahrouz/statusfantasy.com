Status

A companion app for your Yahoo Fantasy leagues, on iPhone and Mac. It shows how your week is going while the games are on.

What it shows

Live scoresYour matchups, updating as plays happen.
Your chance to winEach week's odds, like 59%, and how they move.
TonightWhich of your players are on tonight, and when.
A short summaryA few plain sentences on how your week is going.

How it works

Status doesn't replace Yahoo. Every add, drop, trade and lineup change happens on Yahoo, and Status links you to the right Yahoo page for each one.

You sign in with your own Yahoo account, and you only ever see your own leagues. Status reads them, it never changes them.

Hockey, basketball and football.

Who it's for

Right now, a small group of friends who play in Yahoo leagues together. It's invite-only, free and has no ads. It isn't on the App Store; invites go out through Apple's TestFlight.

Your data

  • Status reads your own Yahoo leagues, with your permission, to show them to you.
  • It's never shared with anyone else, and never sold.
  • It's kept only to show your own week and season history, and deleted when you leave or ask.
